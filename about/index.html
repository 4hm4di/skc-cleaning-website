Who We Are

Our Story & Values

Based proudly in Southeast London, Central London & Kent Area, SKC Cleaning Services was founded on family-inspired values and a commitment to genuine reliability.

We noticed local residents and business owners often struggled to find cleaners who were consistently prompt, thorough, and highly communicative. SKC was built to bridge that gap. Every cleaner in our team is personally vetted, fully insured, and DBS-checked, ensuring you get pristine results along with absolute security.

From eco-friendly cleaning formulas that protect your children and pets, to highly disciplined schedules designed around your routine, we handle the clean so you can enjoy your space.
